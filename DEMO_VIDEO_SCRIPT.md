# 🎬 Demo Video Script - Slop AI

**Target Length**: 3-4 minutes
**Bonus Points**: Live Fire (+5 points)
**Tone**: Casual, confident, like explaining to a friend

---

## 🎯 Video Strategy

### What Judges Want to See:
1. ✅ **The problem** - Why this matters
2. ✅ **Your solution** - What makes it unique
3. ✅ **Live demo** - Working on REAL websites (Live Fire bonus!)
4. ✅ **Results** - Proof it works (accuracy metrics)
5. ✅ **Innovation** - Claim-to-evidence ratio

### Recording Setup:
- **Screen recorder**: OBS Studio (free) or Loom
- **Microphone**: Your laptop mic is fine
- **Browser**: Chrome with extension loaded
- **Websites ready**: Amazon, Medium blog, your test pages

---

## 📝 SCRIPT (Read naturally, don't memorize!)

---

### SCENE 1: INTRO (15 seconds)

**[Show your face or just screen - your choice]**

**YOU SAY**:
> "Hey! I'm [Your Name] and I built Slop AI for the Slop Scan Hackathon. It's a Chrome extension that detects low-quality content - both AI-generated blog posts AND fake reviews. Let me show you how it works."

**[Transition to screen recording]**

---

### SCENE 2: THE PROBLEM (30 seconds)

**[Open a typical AI-generated blog post - use your test page or find a real one]**

**YOU SAY**:
> "Look at this blog post. Sounds professional, right? But read it carefully - it's full of buzzwords like 'leverage', 'comprehensive', 'innovative'. It says a lot but means nothing. This is what I call low information density."

**[Scroll through the content]**

> "And it's everywhere - blog posts, product reviews, social media. It wastes our time. So I built a tool to catch it."

---

### SCENE 3: THE SOLUTION - YOUR INNOVATION (45 seconds)

**[Show extension icon in Chrome toolbar]**

**YOU SAY**:
> "My extension uses 7 different metrics to measure information density. But here's the key innovation - the claim-to-evidence ratio."

**[Open extension popup to show dashboard]**

> "Most AI detectors try to guess if something is AI-written. I don't care about that. I measure if content makes claims without backing them up with evidence - like data, citations, or examples."

**[Point to the metrics on screen]**

> "This is hard to fake. You can't just rewrite AI content - you actually need real evidence. That's what makes this different."

---

### SCENE 4: LIVE DEMO - CONTENT (60 seconds)

**[Navigate to a REAL blog post - Medium or any blog]**

**YOU SAY**:
> "Let me show you on a real website. Here's a blog post I found on [website name]."

**[Click extension icon to scan]**

> "I'll scan it... and look - it flagged 3 paragraphs as low density."

**[Hover over a flagged paragraph to show it faded]**

> "See how they're faded out? I can still read them if I hover, but they're clearly marked."

**[Click on a score to show breakdown]**

> "And if I click the score, it shows me exactly why - high filler words, low compression ratio, unsupported claims. It's completely transparent."

**[Show the dashboard]**

> "The dashboard shows the overall stats - 3 low-density paragraphs, average score 0.68, and the top issues detected."

---

### SCENE 5: LIVE DEMO - REVIEWS (60 seconds)

**[Navigate to Amazon product page with reviews]**

**YOU SAY**:
> "Now here's the cool part - it also works on reviews. Let me go to Amazon."

**[Open a product with many reviews - search for "wireless earbuds" or similar]**

> "I'm on this product page with hundreds of reviews. Let me scan it."

**[Click extension to scan]**

> "Look at this - it detected 5 suspicious reviews and found 2 review clusters."

**[Scroll to show orange-highlighted reviews]**

> "See these reviews with orange borders? They're suspiciously similar - probably bot-generated. The extension groups them into clusters."

**[Click extension popup]**

> "The dashboard shows review-specific stats - total reviews scanned, suspicious ones, and cluster detection. This helps you spot fake reviews before buying."

---

### SCENE 6: THE RESULTS - ACCURACY (30 seconds)

**[Show your accuracy test results - open ACCURACY_METRICS.md or show screenshot]**

**YOU SAY**:
> "Now, does it actually work? I tested it on 20 labeled samples - 10 low-quality, 10 high-quality."

**[Point to the metrics]**

> "The results: 100% precision. That means zero false positives - it never flags good content by mistake. This is critical because I'd rather miss some junk than annoy users by hiding quality content."

**[Show the confusion matrix if you want]**

> "It caught the worst offenders - heavy buzzwords, extreme fluff - while protecting all technical documentation and detailed reviews."

---

### SCENE 7: BONUS ACHIEVEMENTS (20 seconds)

**[Show GitHub repo or just mention it]**

**YOU SAY**:
> "I also built this with professional standards - CI/CD pipeline on GitHub, automated testing, and comprehensive documentation. It covers two hackathon tracks - Content & SEO and Marketplaces - earning the Cross-Track Scanner bonus."

---

### SCENE 8: CLOSING (15 seconds)

**[Back to your face or just screen]**

**YOU SAY**:
> "So that's Slop AI - it detects low-quality content using the claim-to-evidence ratio, works on both articles and reviews, and has 100% precision. The code is on GitHub, and you can try it yourself. Thanks for watching!"

**[Show GitHub link on screen: github.com/PranjaldevX/slope_ai]**

---

## 🎬 RECORDING CHECKLIST

### Before Recording:

- [ ] **Load extension in Chrome**
  - Go to `chrome://extensions/`
  - Enable Developer mode
  - Load unpacked from `extension/dist/`

- [ ] **Prepare websites** (open in tabs):
  - Tab 1: AI-generated blog post (your test page or real one)
  - Tab 2: Amazon product with reviews
  - Tab 3: Your GitHub repo (optional)
  - Tab 4: ACCURACY_METRICS.md (optional)

- [ ] **Test everything works**:
  - Extension scans properly
  - Paragraphs fade correctly
  - Dashboard shows stats
  - Reviews get highlighted

- [ ] **Close unnecessary tabs/windows**
  - Clean desktop
  - Close personal stuff
  - Professional appearance

### Recording Tips:

1. **Speak naturally** - Don't read word-for-word, just follow the flow
2. **Show, don't tell** - Let the extension do the talking
3. **Be confident** - You built something cool!
4. **Pause between sections** - Easier to edit later
5. **If you mess up** - Just pause, restart that section

### After Recording:

- [ ] **Edit out mistakes** (optional - judges don't mind minor errors)
- [ ] **Add text overlays** (optional):
  - "100% Precision" when showing results
  - "Claim-to-Evidence Ratio" when explaining innovation
  - GitHub link at the end
- [ ] **Export as MP4** (1080p recommended)
- [ ] **Upload to YouTube** (unlisted or public)
- [ ] **Add link to README**

## 💡 PRO TIPS

### Make It Personal:

Instead of:
> "This extension detects low-quality content"

Say:
> "I was tired of wasting time on blog posts that say nothing, so I built this"

### Show Enthusiasm:

Instead of:
> "It has 100% precision"

Say:
> "And here's the cool part - 100% precision! It never flags good content by mistake"

### Be Conversational:

Instead of:
> "The claim-to-evidence ratio metric analyzes..."

Say:
> "Here's the key innovation - I check if content makes claims without backing them up"

### Handle Mistakes:

If you mess up while recording:
- Pause for 2 seconds
- Say "Let me try that again"
- Continue from that point
- Edit it out later (or leave it - shows authenticity!)

## 🎥 RECORDING TOOLS

### Free Options:

1. **OBS Studio** (Best for desktop)
   - Download: obsproject.com
   - Records screen + mic
   - Free, professional quality
